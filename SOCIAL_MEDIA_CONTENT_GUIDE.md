# Finko — Social Media Content Guide
### Feature Tutorial Series

> **Purpose:** Short-form educational posts that demonstrate Finko's features step by step.
> **Tone:** Professional but approachable. Practical, not flashy. Spanish-primary, English variant included for each post.
> **Platforms covered:** Instagram (caption + Reel/Story script), LinkedIn, X (Twitter), WhatsApp/Telegram broadcast.
> **Posting cadence:** 2–3 posts/week, rotating through the feature pillars below.

## Video/Reel Production Notes

- **Length:** 20–35 seconds optimal for Instagram Reels. 45–90 sec for LinkedIn video.
- **Format:** 9:16 (vertical) for Reels/Stories. 16:9 for LinkedIn/YouTube.
- **Screen recordings:** Use QuickTime (Mac) at 2× resolution. Zoom into UI elements. Remove cursor blinking pauses.
- **Text overlays:** Use Finko brand colors — `#17c1e8` (cyan) on dark bg `#0a2540`. Font: Helvetica Neue / Inter.
- **Voiceover language:** Spanish primary. English subtitles or separate EN version.
- **Music:** Upbeat, minimal, royalty-free. Avoid lyrics. CapCut or Epidemic Sound.
- **CTA placement:** Last 3 seconds — always include "finko.app" or "Link en bio."
