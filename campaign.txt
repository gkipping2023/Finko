Project: Finko — Property Management System (Marketing / Content Campaign Brief)

Overview
--------
Finko is a property management web app that helps owners and property managers manage leases, tenants, payments, and documents. It centralizes rent collection, payment reporting, receipt generation, property and lease management, and owner/tenant communications — all in a modern, easy-to-use interface.

Objective
---------
- Grow signups among small to mid-size property owners and professional property managers.
- Position Finko as the easiest way to stop scrambling for payments, stay compliant, and automate receipts and tenant communications.
- Drive traffic to `/generate-documents/`, `/properties`, and the registration/signup flows.
-
Primary Audience
----------------
- Independent landlords with 2–50 units looking to professionalize operations.
- Small property management companies and managers responsible for multiple owners.
- Owners who want simple, reliable rent collection + documentation and fewer phone calls from tenants.

Key Benefits / Value Propositions (short)
----------------------------------------
- Save time: automate receipts, payment confirmations and reporting.
- Improve cashflow visibility: accurate payment history and easy-to-generate PDFs you can send or archive.
- Reduce disputes: clear receipts and document history for owners and tenants.
- Simple UX: focused tools for landlords and tenants — no bloated features.

Core Features to Highlight
--------------------------
- Payment reporting & owner confirmation workflow
- Automatic PDF receipt generation and email sending
- Tenant and owner portals (role-specific views)
- Lease management + renewals reminders
- Property-level financial snapshots and exportable reports
